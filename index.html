    What happens after you register

    Schools

    1. We confirm your school and the number of sets you need.
    2. You receive an invoice for bulk books or ARISE sets, with delivery to your country.
    3. Your teachers get the name-chart tool, the game and certificates for each pupil.
    4. Your school is welcomed into the Wonders community and invited to naming ceremonies.

    Families

    1. We confirm your order and the edition language you prefer.
    2. You receive an invoice and delivery details.
    3. Your children read, play and earn their completion certificate.
    4. Your family joins the Wonders community and its events.

    The Wonders Declaration

    Every child has the right to the right name. Children commissioned as Wonders ambassadors carry this message to their schools and communities, grounded in Article 7 of the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child.
